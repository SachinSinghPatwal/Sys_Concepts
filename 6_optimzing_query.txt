
Query optimization is reducing the amount of work the database must perform to answer a query.

Suppose you have:

SELECT *
FROM users
WHERE email = 'dev@example.com';

What work does the database perform?

Very roughly:

Find candidate rows
    ↓
Read rows
    ↓
Filter rows
    ↓
Return results

Optimization means reducing one or more of those steps.

Optimization Hierarchy

When I investigate a slow query, I usually think:

1. What work is being done?
2. Why is it being done?
3. Can we avoid it?
4. If not, can we do it cheaper?
