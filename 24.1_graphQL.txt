Unlike Rest , where server defines what you get , in graphQL the client decides what data it wants

Think of REST as a fixed menu at a restaurant, where the server decides what dishes are available and how they are served. In contrast, GraphQL is like a customizable menu, where the client can specify exactly what ingredients they want in their dish, allowing for more flexibility and efficiency in data retrieval.


unlike REST Multiple endpoints , graphQL exposes a single endpoint /graphql where the client specifies exactly what data it needs
