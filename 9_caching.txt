Caching
Cache are anything that helps you avoid an expensive network I/O , disk and computation 

  1.Api Call to get profile information
  2.Reading a specific line from a file
  3.Doing multiple table joins

  Caches are faster and expensive like (Redis)
  Caches stores any storage that is nearer and helps you avoid something expensive is a cache for you

  Caches are just glorified has table

Some example

1.google News : Most recent news articles are more likely to be access, hence served from cache

2. Auth tokens : Auth are cached in cache to avoid load on database

3. live stream : last 10 min of live stream is cached as it will be accessed the most
